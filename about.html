Our Inception

A Legacy of Service: From 1991 to the Future

Deria Mitra Sangha (দেড়িয়া মিত্র সংঘ) was founded in the year 1991 by a cohort of passionate, visionary local youths. The founding mission was clear: channel youth energy towards physical wellness, sports, cultural enlightenment, and selfless social welfare.

What began as a close-knit neighborhood fraternity has flourished into an established community institution. Over the past three decades, we have remained steadfast in our motto: "In Friendship We Unite, In Service We Prosper".
